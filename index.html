How Active is Australia?

Exploring sport and physical activity participation across age, gender, and geography — and whether Australians are truly active enough.

02

Where is Australia most active?

Participation rates vary across states and territories, with the ACT consistently leading the nation.

Adult participation rate by state and territory

The ACT records the highest participation rate at 92%, while Queensland and South Australia sit just below the national average.

Source: AusPlay National Data Tables, January–December 2025. Australian Sports Commission.

03

Different ages, different sports

The sports Australians choose change dramatically across their lifetime — from team sports in youth to individual activities in older age.

Sport participation intensity by age group

Walking and swimming remain popular across all ages. Basketball and football dominate youth participation but fade with age.

Source: AusPlay By Sport Data Tables, January–December 2025. Australian Sports Commission.

How sport rankings shift with age

Walking rises from rank 3 among teenagers to rank 1 for adults over 55.

Source: AusPlay By Sport Data Tables, 2025.

Organised vs informal participation by age

Younger Australians favour organised sport; older adults prefer informal activity.

Source: AusPlay National Data Tables, 2025.

04

Active — but is it enough?

Participation rates tell only half the story. Most Australians fall short of national physical activity guidelines.

Participation rate vs. meeting physical activity guidelines, by age group

While 84% of Australians participate in some activity, only around 20% meet the recommended guidelines for both aerobic and muscle-strengthening activity.

Source: AusPlay 2025 (participation); AIHW Physical Activity Data Tables 2022 (guidelines).

Who meets physical activity guidelines?

Young adults aged 18–24 are the most likely to meet guidelines; older adults fall further behind.

Source: AIHW Physical Activity Data Tables, 2022.

Meeting guidelines over time, 2007–2022

The proportion of Australians meeting activity guidelines has remained stubbornly low over 15 years.

Source: AIHW Physical Activity Data Tables, 2022.

05

What drives — and stops — us?

Understanding why Australians participate, and what barriers prevent others, reveals a complex picture shaped by age and gender.

Motivations for participation

Physical health is the top motivator across all age groups, but fun and enjoyment dominate for younger Australians.

Source: AusPlay National Data Tables, 2025.

Barriers to participation: gender differences

Women are more likely to cite poor health, disability and cost as barriers; men more often report laziness.

Source: AusPlay National Data Tables, 2025.
